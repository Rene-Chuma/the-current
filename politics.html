ILLOVO SUGAR MALAWI HAS DONATED K5 MILLION FOR THE UPCOMING SPORTS FESTIVAL FOR MALAWIAN SECURITY AGENTS


Illovo Sugar Malawi has donated K5 million towards the upcoming sports festival for Malawi’s security agents. The financial contribution is expected to support the organisation of the event, which brings together members of the country’s security services through sporting activities. The donation highlights the company’s support for sports and activities that promote interaction among security personnel. Further details about the festival, including its dates and participating security agencies, are yet to be confirmed.
